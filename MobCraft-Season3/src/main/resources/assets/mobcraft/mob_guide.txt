## The Mob Guide, shown from the pause menu and the title screen.
## "# TYPE" starts a mob, using its MobType name. "* " is a small heading,
## "- " is an entry. "Name, key: text" shows the name and key in color.
## R, G and V are the default ability keys and U the ultimate. Players can
## change them in Controls. Lines starting with ## don't show.
# RABBIT
- Passive: Speed II, Jump Boost III, half size, instant respawn
- Escorts: R, or left-click while holding a rabbit's foot: up to 5 killer rabbits, 15.5s cooldown, 25 alive at once. They follow, defend and attack per the Mob Behavior settings, and take RTS orders.
- Home: Desert, Snowy Plains, Snowy Taiga: escorts get Speed I and half of Strength I ("The Rabbit King Must Protect"). Killer rabbits never flee ("Keep Fighting for the King").
- Double jump: press jump in midair to jump again, 3 energy each. As many as your energy allows before you land.
- No fall damage
- Curse: only one midair jump before you land, and can't self-give raw rabbit. Raw rabbit already held stays.
- Ultimate, U: Killer Stampede. Up to 10 killer rabbits at once, no rabbit's foot needed, still 25 at most. Every escort within 32 blocks gets Strength I and Speed II for 20s.
# IRON_GOLEM
- Passive: Resistance I, +3 hearts attack damage, 1.4 size, knockback immunity, no fall damage, Slowness I. Mace smash does half damage.
- Golem Toss, R: your next empty-hand hit launches the target upward. A tossed player is stunned for 2s: no moving, jumping or attacking. 15 energy, 8s cooldown.
- Iron Repair: right-click an iron ingot: heal 4 hearts, 10s cooldown. Teammates can right-click you with an ingot to repair you.
- Allies: villagers and iron golems
- Home: Villages: Resistance II and Regeneration I
- Curse, Rusted: loses Resistance and knockback immunity, Slowness II
- Ultimate, U: Earthquake. You slam the ground. Enemies within 7 blocks take 4 hearts and are thrown up. Players are stunned for 3s, mobs slowed to a crawl.
# SKELETON
- Self-give: bow with Power III and Curse of Vanishing, arrows, bones
- Biome arrows: where he fires decides the effect: Desert Weakness 30s, snowy biomes Slowness 30s, swamps Poison 4s
- Lock On, R: your next 3 arrows home in. 25 energy, 20s cooldown.
- Skeleton Horse, G: a saddled skeleton horse you ride at once. 40 energy, 2:00 cooldown. Calling a new one sends the old one away.
- Undead: poison immune, Instant Damage heals, Instant Health hurts, Smite does bonus damage, burns in sunlight without a helmet, Weakness I within 8 blocks of a wolf
- Allies: skeletons, strays, bogged
- Home: Soul Sand Valley: Speed I and double energy regen
- Curse: no Lock On, no self-given arrows
- Ultimate, U: Arrow Rain. Arrows rain down where you look, up to 48 blocks away: 48 arrows over 3 seconds across 5 blocks. They can't be picked up.
# AXOLOTL
- Passive: Water Breathing, Dolphin's Grace, instant respawn. Regeneration II only in water or rain.
- Drying out: after 5 minutes dry, takes half a heart every 2 seconds until wet
- Play Dead: R, in water: 10s frozen, can't attack, mobs lose interest, Regeneration III. 25 energy, 45s cooldown.
- Call the Pod: G, in water: 3 axolotls you can command in RTS. 30 energy, 30s cooldown.
- Hunting Partner: a teammate who kills something you hurt in the last 10s gets Regeneration I for 5s and loses Mining Fatigue
- Allies: axolotls
- Home: Lush Caves: double energy regen
- Curse: dry everywhere, no Regeneration, drying runs underwater, no pod
- Ultimate, U: Tidal Surge. You and teammates within 10 blocks get Regeneration III and Resistance I for 10s. Enemies within 10 blocks are washed back and slowed for 8s.
# PIGLIN
- Passive: Strength I. Inside a Bastion Remnant: Strength II and Resistance I.
- Allies: piglins and piglin brutes, so mining gold never angers them
- Gold Rush, R: Speed II for 8s. 20 energy, 30s cooldown.
- Crossbow, G: a crossbow for 1:00 with Piercing V, Multishot I and Curse of Vanishing. It vanishes if you die. 2:00 cooldown after it's gone.
- Curse: Zombification: no Strength, can't self-give gold or crying obsidian
- Ultimate, U: Bastion Rally. Strength II, Resistance I and Speed II for 20s, and 3 piglin brutes fight for you for 45s. They don't zombify and take RTS orders.
# GHAST
- Passive: slow flight, Fire Resistance, Regeneration I only in the Nether
- Fireball, R: one ghast fireball. 15 energy, 3s cooldown. Can be punched back.
- Barrage, G: three fireballs in a spread. 45 energy, 30s cooldown.
- Ferry: up to 4 teammates (same /team) can right-click you to ride along beside you. Sneak to get off.
- Allies: ghasts and happy ghasts
- Home: Nether Wastes and Soul Sand Valley: faster flight, double energy regen
- Curse: Dried Out: can't fly or shoot. Standing in water ends it early.
- Ultimate, U: Hellfire. Two volleys of 5 fireballs fanned out where you look, a second apart.
# BAT
- Passive: half size, flight at about sprint speed, instant respawn
- Lifesteal: hitting a player heals you for a quarter of the damage
- Confuse: R primes it. Your next hit on a player stops them moving and attacking for 25s and jams their RTS. A mob you hit can't get new RTS orders for 25s but keeps its current one. 35 energy, 1:30 cooldown. Stays primed until a hit lands, cleared when you log out.
- Echolocation: G, only in caves: every living thing within 24 blocks glows on your screen for 10s. 15 energy, 10s cooldown.
- Curse: Blinded: Blindness the whole time, but Echolocation is always on, anywhere, open air or cave. Every living thing within 24 blocks glows on your screen and gets a vibration marker drawn over the blindness. Mods that hide Blindness don't help.
- Ultimate, U: Night Swarm. 12 bats burst out around you for 10s. Every enemy within 14 blocks glows for 15s, and enemy players are blinded for 6s.
# CREEPER
- Gunpowder Blast, R: explode where you stand without dying: power 3, or 6 while charged. Your own blast never hurts you. 40 energy, 20s cooldown.
- Self Charge, G: become a charged creeper for 30s: your next blast is double power and you glow. 30 energy, 1:00 cooldown.
- Leaf Hide, V: while touching leaves, turn invisible for up to 10s. Ends if you hit something or leave the leaves. 20 energy, 30s cooldown.
- Cats: within 6 blocks of a cat or ocelot you're slowed and can't blast
- Allies: creepers
- Curse: no Gunpowder Blast
- Ultimate, U: Supernova. You glow and hiss for 1.5s, then blow up at power 7.5, over twice a charged Gunpowder Blast. It never hurts you. A cat nearby stops it.
# SILVERFISH
- Passive: tiny, Haste II
- Stone Swim, R: toggle: sink into the stone you're standing on or facing and swim through it in any direction. You can pass through every kind of stone, gravel and stone ores, plus air, nothing else. Inside stone you float, so jump and sneak move you up and down. In open air you fall and walk like normal until you're back in stone. You're hidden inside the rock, can't attack or use items, and don't regenerate energy. 10 energy to enter. Press R again to climb out at the nearest open spot.
- Infest, Sneak+R: stone-type blocks within 3 blocks turn into infested blocks that release silverfish when broken. Up to 24 blocks. 20 energy, 20s cooldown.
- Summon Silverfish, G: 3 silverfish you can command in RTS. 12 alive at most. 25 energy, 30s cooldown.
- Curse Attack, V: primes your next hit: Mining Fatigue II and Weakness I for 15s. 20 energy, 20s cooldown.
- Allies: silverfish and endermites
- Curse: no Stone Swim, Infest or summoning
- Ultimate, U: Infestation. 8 silverfish swarm out for 1:00, on top of your usual 12, and take RTS orders. Enemies within 8 blocks get Mining Fatigue II and Weakness I for 15s.
# EVOKER
- Fangs, R with a totem in your main hand: a line of fangs where you look. Sneak+R: a ring of fangs around you. 5 energy, 1s cooldown.
- Summon Vexes, G: 2 second charge, then 3 vexes that attack whoever you hit and can be commanded in RTS. They fade after 60s. 30 energy, 20s cooldown, 9 alive at most.
- Raid Captain: Put an ominous banner on your head (right-click it, only the Evoker can) and you become a raid captain for 1:00. Pillagers, vindicators, evokers, illusioners, ravagers and witches within 32 blocks won't attack you, go after whatever you hit or whatever hits you, and get Strength I and Speed I within 16 blocks. Whoever kills you while you're captain gets Bad Omen. Taking the banner off ends it. 35 energy, 2:30 cooldown after the minute.
- Self-give: totems only out of combat (15s since you last hit or were hit), emeralds any time
- Allies: evokers and vexes. No vex can ever hurt you.
- Bells: within 50 blocks of a bell you glow
- Home: Woodland Mansion: take 10% less damage
- Curse: no totem self-give
- Ultimate, U: Fang Storm. Three rings of fangs burst out around you one after another, 42 fangs in all, and 3 vexes join you (up to the usual 9).
# ENDERMAN
- Teleport, R: to the block you're looking at, up to 32 blocks away. 15 energy, 3s cooldown.
- Auto Dodge: passive: a projectile that would hit you teleports you up to 8 blocks away instead. 5 energy each time. Switch off in /mobcraftsettings.
- Water: water and rain hurt you, half a heart a second
- Ender pearls: no pearl damage
- Allies: endermen and endermites
- Curse: no Teleport and no Auto Dodge
- Ultimate, U: Void Rift. Every enemy within 16 blocks is teleported to a random spot 8 to 16 blocks from where they stood, with Nausea for 8s. Players are blinded for 3s too.
# VEX
- Passive: vanilla vex size, flight at walking speed, no fall damage
- Phase, R: toggle: fly through blocks. Costs 2 energy a second. You can't attack, open containers or use items while phasing. Ends by itself at 0 energy. If you end inside a block you're pushed to the nearest open space.
- Charge, G: dash forward and strike whatever's in front of you within 6 blocks with your held weapon. 15 energy, 6s cooldown.
- Borrowed Time: at 0 energy you take half a heart every 3s until energy recovers
- Allies: vexes and evokers
- Home: Woodland Mansion: double energy regen
- Curse: can't phase or fly
- Ultimate, U: Spectral Legion. 5 vexes fight for you for 30s and take RTS orders, and you get Strength I and Speed I for 10s.
# SHULKER
- Shell defense: stand still for 2s: Resistance I (II in the End) and projectiles bounce off you back at whoever shot them
- Blocking, R: close your shell for 5s: Resistance II and Blindness (no Blindness in the End), then Slowness I for 3s. 10 energy, 15s cooldown.
- Teleport, G: to a random safe spot within 8 blocks. 5s cooldown.
- Shulker Bullet, V: at whatever you're looking at within 32 blocks. 5 energy, 2s cooldown. Turns off shell defense for 5s.
- Elytra Gamble, Sneak+V: 1 in 50 chance at an Elytra. 50 energy a roll.
- RTS Teleport: in RTS mode with nothing selected, click a shulker to teleport next to it, or click anywhere in an End City to teleport there. 8 energy, no range limit.
- Allies: endermen and shulkers
- Drops: Elytra too, if killed with a Looting weapon
- Ultimate, U: Bullet Storm. Shulker bullets at up to 10 enemies within 24 blocks, more bullets each when there are fewer targets (3 at most). Resistance II for 5s.
# WITHER
- Flight: double-tap jump to fly, a bit faster than walking
- Nether: Speed I anywhere in the Nether, Speed II within 200 blocks of a fortress, Strength I only inside a fortress
- Undead: immune to Wither and poison, Instant Damage heals and Instant Health hurts
- Black Skull, R: a wither skull where you look. 10 energy, 1s cooldown.
- Blue Skull, Sneak+R: a dangerous blue skull that breaks blocks it hits. 25 energy, 8s cooldown.
- Charge, G: dash forward. Anything you ram takes 6 damage and Wither II for 5s. 25 energy, 15s cooldown.
- Block Destruction, V: breaks the blocks around you, like the Wither breaking out. Bedrock and other wither-proof blocks stay. 30 energy, 20s cooldown.
- Allies: wither skeletons
- Curse: no skulls
- Ultimate, U: Skull Storm. 16 black skulls fly out in a ring and 3 blue skulls go where you look. Enemies within 10 blocks get Wither II for 10s.
# ILLUSIONER
- Clones, R: turn invisible for 8s while 3 illusioner clones appear around you and fight for you. They vanish after 15s. 30 energy, 30s cooldown.
- Arrow Burst, G: 8 arrows fanned out where you look. 20 energy, 10s cooldown.
- Blindness, V: whatever you're looking at within 24 blocks is blinded for 6s. 15 energy, 20s cooldown.
- Allies: illagers: pillagers, vindicators, evokers, illusioners
- Curse: no Clones
- Ultimate, U: Hall of Mirrors. 6 clones appear around you while you turn invisible for 10s, and every enemy player within 20 blocks is blinded for 6s.
# WARDEN
- Passive: +9 attack damage, Resistance I, Fire Resistance
- Sonic Shriek, R: a sonic boom 15 blocks ahead, through walls and armor, 10 damage and knockback. 30 energy, 10s cooldown.
- Rage, Sneak+R: Strength I and Speed I for 15s. 40 energy, 1:00 cooldown.
- Echolocation, G: for 10s, white ghosts show where everything nearby was 1s ago. Sneaking makes no vibration, so it leaves none. 15 energy, 20s cooldown.
- Sniffing, Sneak+G: for 10s, red ghosts show where everything nearby was 10s ago. Sneaking doesn't hide scent. 15 energy, 20s cooldown.
- Burrow, V: dig into the ground for up to 8s: invisible, can't be hurt, can't move or attack, mobs lose track of you, Regeneration I. Press V again to come up early. Coming up knocks nearby things back. 25 energy, 30s cooldown.
- Sculk Teleport, Sneak+V: jump to the sculk block you're looking at, up to 48 blocks away. 20 energy, 15s cooldown.
- Energy drain: every hit you give or take costs 1 energy
- Exhaustion: at 0 energy you're cursed for 1:00
- Allies: wardens, always, even if you hit them
- Sculk: shriekers ignore you, so you never summon wardens or get Darkness from them
- Curse: no abilities, passives stay
- Ultimate, U: Sculk Eruption. The ground around you turns to sculk and every enemy on it is thrown into the air, then slammed down hard. They take 6 hearts that armor doesn't stop, their boots lose a third of their durability, and players are stunned for 3 seconds. The sculk fades after 30 seconds.
# VILLAGER
* Powered up
- Money: his energy. Up to 100 quadrillion, earning 100 trillion a second. Shown in the energy bar.
- RTS: he can command every mob, not just his own.
- Warden Bodyguards, R: summons 2 Wardens that follow and fight for him. 1 quadrillion, 30s cooldown, up to 4 at once.
- Jeff's Menu, G: every item in the game, his own creative menu.
- Commands: he's a server operator while powered up.
## Hidden: * Shut down
## Hidden: - A plain Villager with normal energy. Gives himself emeralds, but only within 5 blocks of a crafting table or a villager job block. Raid mobs (pillagers, vindicators, evokers, illusioners, ravagers, witches) do half again as much damage to him.
- Allies: villagers and iron golems, either way.
- Ultimate, U: Call the Guard. 3 iron golems come to defend you for 1:00 and take RTS orders, powered up or not.
# ENDER_DRAGON
- Energy: 100, 1 per second. Double in the End.
- Flight: sprinting speed, 1 energy per second while flying (instead of regenerating). Out of energy, she can't fly again until she has 10. Taking damage grounds her for 3s.
- Immunities: Wither and Slow Falling. Fire Resistance and Water Breathing.
- Crystals: the nearest end crystal within 24 blocks beams at her and heals her: Regeneration II in the End, Regeneration I anywhere else.
- Dragon Breath, R: a dragon fireball where she looks, leaving a damaging purple cloud that never hurts her. 30 energy, 20s cooldown.
- Wing Buffet, G: knocks back players within 6 blocks. 20 energy, 12s cooldown.
- The Egg: starts with the dragon egg. Self-give one only without one in her inventory, 10:00 cooldown. Never drops it on death.
- Portals: self-give end portal frames, placeable only in the End or a stronghold. Once she has been to the End, she can break end portal frames by punching them, getting the frame back.
- Self-give: dragon's breath, 4 at a time, out of combat only
- Home: the End: Strength I and double energy regen
- Allies: endermen, endermites and shulkers
- Curse: can't fly, no crystal regen, can't spawn an egg
- Drops: 64 dragon's breath and a dragon head, never the egg
- The End: while someone is the Ender Dragon, no dragon spawns and all 20 gateways open. Switching off the Ender Dragon turns that off again and takes the egg away.
- Ultimate, U: Dragon's Fury. 5 dragon fireballs in a wide spread where she looks, and every enemy within 10 blocks is blown far back.
# BLAZE
- Energy: 100, 1 per second
- Passives: Fire Resistance, slow hover flight
- Weaknesses: water and rain hurt you, half a heart a second. Snowballs do 3 damage, like they do to real blazes. No flying in water.
- Combat Phase: starts on its own when you hit a player or one hits you. Flames and smoke pour off you, your melee hits set targets on fire, and the Fireballs cooldown is halved. But your energy stops regenerating and water hurts twice as much. Ends after 10s with no hits either way (15s at home).
- Fireballs, R: 3 small fireballs in quick succession where you look. 15 energy, 4s cooldown (2s in Combat Phase).
- Fire Shield, G: for 6s, anyone who hits you in melee is set on fire and you take half melee damage. 30 energy, 30s cooldown.
- Blaze Burst, V: Combat Phase only. A ring of fire throws back players within 4 blocks and sets them on fire. 35 energy, 45s cooldown.
- Home: Nether Fortress: Strength I, double energy regen, and Combat Phase lasts 15s.
- Allies: blazes
- Self-give: blaze rods
- Curse: Snuffed Out, 3:00: no Combat Phase, no Fireballs, no flying, no self-given blaze rods.
- Drops: 16 blaze rods and 32 blaze powder
- Ultimate, U: Inferno. Three rings of 16 small fireballs fly out from you half a second apart. Enemies within 6 blocks catch fire for 8s, and Combat Phase starts.
# ELDER_GUARDIAN
- Water: Dolphin's Grace, Water Breathing, Night Vision while under.
- Land: Slowness I and no sprinting.
- Size: 1.2 times normal.
- Spikes: standing still, melee attackers take 1 heart back per hit.
- Laser Beam, R: locks onto the player you're looking at (24 blocks), charges 2s while you keep them in sight, then 6 damage through armor. Losing sight cancels it. 30 energy, 15s cooldown.
- Elder Curse, G: enemy players within 20 blocks (40 in a monument) see the ghost face and get Mining Fatigue III for 30s. 40 energy, 2:00 cooldown.
- Guardians, V: 2 guardians for RTS, in water only, 6 at most. 35 energy, 45s cooldown.
- Home: Ocean Monument: Resistance I, double energy regen.
- Allies: guardians, elder guardians, drowned.
- Conduits: within 8 blocks of an active conduit, in water or rain: 2 hearts every 2 seconds.
- Curse, Beached: no swim speed, no Laser Beam, spikes off.
- Drops: prismarine shards and wet sponges.
- Ultimate, U: Abyssal Gaze. Every enemy within 32 blocks (48 in a monument) sees the ghost face, takes 4 hearts of magic damage, and gets Mining Fatigue III for 1:00 and Slowness II for 6s.
# BREEZE
- Passives: no fall damage, Jump Boost II. Arrows and tridents bounce off, except in water. Takes 1.5 times the knockback.
- Gust, R: a wind charge where you look. 10 energy, 2s cooldown.
- Leap, G: launches you high and forward. 15 energy, 6s cooldown.
- Whirlwind, V: players within 5 blocks are thrown up and away. 35 energy, 30s cooldown.
- Home: Trial Chambers: Speed I and double energy regen.
- Allies: breezes.
- Self-give: wind charges.
- Curse, Still Air: no Leap, nothing bounces off, no self-given wind charges.
- Drops: 4 breeze rods and 32 wind charges.
- Ultimate, U: Tempest. 8 wind charges fly out in a ring, enemies within 10 blocks are thrown high into the air, and you're lifted up with them.
